Quota Telemetry

Two readings of one question: how much of the weekly window does each 5-hour session burn — and when am I forced to stop? Simulated data; real capture is one day deep.

01

Session vs Week

Every session is a rectangle: width = how much of its 5-hour window it used, height = how much of the weekly window it ate. Stack a week and the tower's height is that week's weekly burn. A tall-narrow block is a quiet session that still cost the week dearly; a wide-flat block is an intense sprint that barely dented the budget. Rectangles touching the right wall hit the 5-hour ceiling (you had to wait it out); the red band is the weekly ceiling; amber hatch is spend that ran on credits past 100%.

02

Week Burn-up

One panel per week, all on the same 0→100% scale so the shapes compare directly — usage only ever climbs and can't pass 100% (spillover becomes credits). The faint diagonal is an even-pace line: a curve above it is burning faster than the week can afford; below it you're holding back. This is the "50% and it's Tuesday — go" versus "72% and it's Thursday — hold" read, and reading left→right shows whether your pacing is drifting. Dots are sessions; the red zone is past 90%; amber breaks above the ceiling as credit spend.
